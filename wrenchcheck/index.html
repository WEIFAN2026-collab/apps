I subscribed to WrenchCheck Pro, but my limit did not unlock.

Open Settings in WrenchCheck and tap Restore Purchases while signed in with the same Apple Account you used to subscribe.

How do I cancel or manage my subscription?

Open the iPhone Settings app, tap your Apple Account, then tap Subscriptions and select WrenchCheck.

How do I turn off AI data sharing?

Open Settings inside WrenchCheck to review or revoke your AI processing consent at any time.

Will I lose my garage records if I switch iPhones?

Your garage assets and logs are stored on your iPhone. Transferring via iCloud or encrypted computer backup preserves them.
